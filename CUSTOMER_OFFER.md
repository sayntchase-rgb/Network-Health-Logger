# Network Health Diagnostic — Introductory Offer

## Is your internet unreliable, but you cannot prove when or how often it fails?

The **Network Health Diagnostic** records network availability and latency over time and turns the results into a clear report you can use for troubleshooting, equipment decisions, or conversations with your ISP.

## What you receive

- Network-health monitoring over an agreed test period
- Timestamped connectivity history
- Uptime estimate
- Observed failed-check / probe-loss summary
- Minimum, average, and maximum latency
- HEALTHY / DEGRADED / DOWN counts
- Outage-event summary
- Plain-language findings
- Suggested next troubleshooting steps
- Customer-readable HTML report
- TXT report and structured CSV history

## Good fit if you experience

- Internet that drops randomly
- Video calls that freeze or disconnect
- Cloud software or online payments losing connection
- Booking, point-of-sale, or office systems becoming unreliable
- Security cameras or connected devices going offline
- Unexplained latency spikes
- A problem that disappears before support can see it

## What the diagnostic answers

The service is designed to help establish:

1. **When** connectivity problems occurred
2. **How often** the monitored target became unreachable
3. Whether **latency increased** during the observation period
4. Whether the evidence points to the need for deeper local-network or ISP troubleshooting

## What it does not include

This is not a penetration test, cybersecurity audit, Wi-Fi RF survey, ISP SLA certification, packet-capture service, or guarantee of root-cause identification.

No credentials are collected and no router, firewall, or customer equipment changes are made without separate approval.

## How it works

1. Brief intake: describe the problem and affected devices
2. Choose an appropriate authorized monitoring target
3. Collect network-health observations over an agreed period
4. Generate and review the diagnostic report
5. Identify reasonable next troubleshooting steps based on measured evidence

## Current goal

The service is intentionally small. The goal is to provide useful evidence before recommending unnecessary hardware replacement, ISP escalation, or deeper troubleshooting.
